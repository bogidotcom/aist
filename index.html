Compute payout

Withdraw your AI compute earnings

Your GPU takes AI jobs and gets paid in compute coins. This is where those coins turn into something your bank, your wallet or your landlord accepts.

Run the jobs

Your node settles finished AI compute work in KGST or a regional compute coin — aiGEL, aiETB, aiBTN. Nothing to claim, nothing to unstake.

List the payout

Post an ask on the P2P book at your own price, or hit a standing bid if you want it gone today. Partial fills are normal.

USDT, BTC, ETH, SOL or a fiat method. Escrow releases the moment the quote side lands, typically inside the 15-minute window.

P2P liquidity

Be the book, not the taker

Every taker who wants out right now pays someone for the privilege. Provide the float that lets them, and that payment is your yield — earned on inventory you were holding anyway.

Quote both sides

Post a bid under the mid and an ask above it on the same pair. You are not calling direction — you are selling immediacy, and collecting the spread each time the book round-trips.

Escrow carries the trust

The base side locks the moment an order is selected and stays locked for 15 minutes. You are never fronting an unknown counterparty on good faith.

Get paid in flow

Thin pairs pay the widest edge and fill slowly; KGST-USDT pays the least and fills constantly. Split your float across methods and let the fills come to you.
