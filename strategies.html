    Before your first trade

    Four things decide whether a spread is real profit or just a number on a screen.

    Rules of thumb

    1. Price the round trip, not the leg. A 1.4% spread with two on-chain fees and a bank charge can net under zero on small size.
    2. Escrow runs 15 minutes. If a leg cannot finish inside that window, it is not one trade — it is two positions.
    3. Depth beats price. The best quote for 200 KGST tells you nothing about where 200,000 KGST actually fills.
    4. Keep float on both sides. An opportunity you have to fund first is usually gone by the time the funding lands.

    Worked example

    spread   1.40%  on 1,000 USDT
gross    +14.00 USDT
─────────────────────────────
TRC20 out        -1.00
TRC20 in         -1.00
bank fee         -2.30
─────────────────────────────
net      +9.70 USDT  (0.97%)
